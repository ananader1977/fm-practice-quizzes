Reference & Instructions

How to Use This Book & Abbreviations

Study Workflow

  1. Work through each section untimed. Select one best answer per question before looking at the section key. Mark any items you guessed.
  2. Check your answers at the end of the section. Read the explanations for missed and guessed items, then review the topics that caused errors.
  3. Proceed to the next section once you achieve a passing score (above 80%).
  4. Retake a mixed set drawn from several sections without topic cues to check consistency.

Practice Score Bands

Practice Score What to Do Next
Above 80% (80%+) Likely ready on the topics practiced. Proceed to the next section or retake a mixed set.
60% to 80% Targeted review. Focus on missed topics, then retake those sections.
Below 60% Revisit fundamentals before attempting another mixed set.
